## Diagnosis

1. **Carousels on a 4-follower account do not travel.** Instagram shows a carousel to followers first, and we have almost none. Six of seven carousels reached 2–6 accounts. Producing 2 carousels per day is effort with almost no return.
2. **Every Reel gets a test batch of about 100 non-followers, then fails the test.** Skip rate is 77–82% and average watch is 2–3 s, so Instagram stops distributing. The bottleneck is the first 2 seconds, not the topic.
3. **The best Reel (deposito, 7.6 s) opened with a single big number ("6%").** The weakest opened with a question sentence being written. A big number shown at once holds; a sentence that is still being written loses.
4. **Videos are too long for a cold audience (63–68 s).** The point arrives after 20 s, and nobody gets there. Zero comments and zero saves follow from that.
5. **Gold is the only post that earned a share and a follow.** It used a real price and a concrete loss that people can check themselves.

## Strategy (target: get Instagram to push us beyond the test batch)

### A. Change the content mix: Reels first
- **Per day: 2 Reels + 1 carousel** (was 1 Reel + 2 carousels).
- TTS capacity allows it: two models with 10 requests each per day, and a short Reel needs 3–4 requests.
- Slots:
  - 07:00 carousel.
  - 12:00 Reels.
  - 19:00 Reels.

### B. Reels v3 format (from 19 Nov)
- **15–25 seconds**: 3 blocks only (hook number, the one fact plus its effect on your wallet, one action plus a one-word CTA).
- **Frame 0**: one giant number or rupiah figure taking a third of the screen, already drawn, with movement within 0.3 s (strike-through, coin dropping, number counting down) and a short sound effect at 0 s.
- **Spoken hook = the number** (e.g. "Enam ribu lima ratus. Hilang tiap transfer.").
- A visual change every 1.5–2 s; no camera move longer than 0.4 s.
- Ends on the hook board, so it loops.

### C. Hook test (each Reel is labelled with its hook type)
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| A. Shock number | "Rp83 ribu. Hilang tiap bulan." |
| B. Direct callout | "Kamu yang bayar kartu kredit minimum, stop dulu." |
| C. Myth-bust | "Kemasan besar itu lebih mahal." |
| D. Real price / check yourself | "Harga emas hari ini: jual Rp2,51 jt, buyback Rp2,31 jt." |

After 4 days, keep the 2 types with the lowest skip rate.

### D. Topics: concrete numbers people can check
Priority: everyday hidden fees and cuts (admin fees, transfer fees, ATM, late fees, interest, prices of gold, fuel and electricity), plus scam alerts. Defer abstract macro topics until we have an audience.

### E. Distribution (requires the user, about 15 minutes a day)
For a new account, the strongest lever that automation cannot replace safely:
- Leave 5–10 useful comments a day on posts by large Indonesian finance accounts, from the @intipdompet account.
- Reply to every comment within an hour.
- Pin the 3 best posts.
- Make the bio name the value plainly: "Biaya tersembunyi & jebakan uang sehari-hari, dihitung pakai angka."

### F. Measurement
- Daily (19:25 trigger): skip rate, average watch, reach and shares per post.
- Every 2 days: change the hook style based on data.

## 7-day targets (realistic for an account with 4 followers)
| Metric | Now | Target 22 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Reels skip rate (median) | 81% | < 65% |
| Reels average watch | 2–3 s | ≥ 5 s |
| Best Reel reach | 138 | ≥ 1,000 |
| Followers | 4 | ≥ 50 |
| Comments in total | 0 | ≥ 10 |

The targets are deliberately modest. Most accounts need 3–6 weeks before the algorithm settles; 7 days is enough to prove whether the hook improvements work (skip rate and watch time), not to build a large audience.
